Our Story

Junk removal, done the way it should be.

JunkMatter is a locally operated junk removal company serving homes and businesses across the Greater Toronto Area. We saw too many people dealing with hidden fees, no-shows, and hauling companies that didn't take disposal seriously — so we built a service around three things: honest pricing, reliable scheduling, and responsible disposal.

Every job starts with a clear quote and ends with a swept, clear space. In between, our crew sorts what we collect — usable furniture and goods are routed to local donation partners where possible, recyclables are separated, and only what's left goes to landfill.
