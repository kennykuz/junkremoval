01 Our Story

Built by someone tired of bad service.

ClearSpace was founded in 2014 after our founder spent a weekend chasing down a junk hauler who never showed. We started with one truck, a clear promise — show up on time, quote a fair price, and follow through — and grew from there.

Today we run a growing fleet across the Greater Toronto Area, but the promise hasn't changed. Every crew is trained the same way, every quote follows the same transparent pricing, and every load gets sorted for donation and recycling before anything reaches a landfill.
