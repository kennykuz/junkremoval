FAQ

Common Questions

Most bookings made before noon can be completed the same day. Next-day appointments are always available, and we offer 2-hour arrival windows so you're not stuck waiting all day.

We price by how much space your items take up in our truck, not by weight or item count. Your crew leader shows you the exact price before any work begins — you can always say no, free of charge.

We can't accept hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, asbestos, or propane tanks. Items such as tires, mattresses, and e-waste are accepted for a small additional disposal fee due to specialized recycling requirements.

We sort every load at our facility — usable furniture and goods are donated to local charities, metals and electronics are recycled, and only what's left goes to a licensed disposal facility.
