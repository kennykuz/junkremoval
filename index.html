Who We Are

Junk Removal That Respects Your Time & Your Property

JunkMatter was built around a simple idea: getting rid of junk shouldn't be a hassle. We show up when we say we will, quote a price you can trust before we start, and treat your home or business the way we'd want ours treated.

Wherever possible, we sort what we collect — usable furniture and goods go to local donation partners, metal and electronics get recycled, and only what's left goes to a landfill.
