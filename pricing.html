Pricing

We don't post one-size-fits-all prices, because no two jobs are the same. Here's what actually determines your quote.

What Determines Your Price

Five factors go into every quote.

Every job is different, so we price based on the actual work involved rather than a flat rate. Send us a few details (and photos, if you can) and we'll get back to you with a clear, upfront number.

Volume Of Junk

How much space your items take up in the truck — a single item versus a full load.

Type Of Materials

General household junk, appliances, and construction debris are priced differently.

Location

Where the job is and how far it is from our service routes.

Accessibility

Stairs, elevators, and distance from your items to the truck all factor in.

Labor Required

How much lifting, sorting, and disposal work the job actually takes.
